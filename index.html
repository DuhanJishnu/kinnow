Three moves. No menus.

1

Drag

Pick up any file in Windows Explorer like you normally would.

2

Shift

Tap Shift mid-drag. The wheel opens right at your cursor, clamped to the screen edges.

3

Drop

Release over a format. A small toast appears; click it to jump straight to the new file.

Small tool. Big manners.
