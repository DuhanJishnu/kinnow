Designed for Speed & Simplicity

Built as an intuitive local companion that stays out of your way until you need it.

⚡

Drag + Shift Trigger

Works directly inside Windows Explorer. Start dragging any file, press Shift, and the radial menu appears under your cursor.

🔒

100% Local & Offline

Zero cloud uploads. Your confidential documents, photos, and media are processed completely on your own machine.

📁

Click to Reveal

A completion toast confirms your conversion. Click it to immediately highlight the new file in Windows Explorer.

🛡️

Non-Destructive

Never overwrites your originals. Smart automatic collision handling names duplicate targets name (1).ext.

🖥️

Edge Clamping

Intelligently detects screen boundaries and multi-monitor setups so the wheel never gets cut off at display edges.

📦

Zero Installation

A purely portable executable. No setup wizards, registry entries, or administrator privileges required.

Supported Formats

Conversions powered by trusted local libraries: Pillow, PyMuPDF, and FFmpeg.
